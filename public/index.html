Growth Potential (GP) estimates how fast the grass is growing today, from 0-100%, based on temperature. A customer's lawn is flagged "ready to mow" once their accumulated GP since the last visit crosses the threshold above - tune the threshold up or down based on how it matches what you see in the yard.

          Lawn Treatment Windows

          Growing Degree Days (GDD50) this year: –

          Timed by Growing Degree Days (GDD50, base 50°F accumulated since Jan 1) - the standard way turf professionals time preemergent herbicides, since it tracks accumulated heat instead of a fixed calendar date. Fall fertilizing follows the calendar instead, since that's how cool-season lawn feeding is actually timed. Adjust the GDD50 windows above if your local extension office recommends different numbers.
